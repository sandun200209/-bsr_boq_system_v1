================================================================================
          BSR RATE HUB - SRI LANKA BOQ & MULTI-SECTOR RATE SYSTEM
                     COMPLETE INSTALLATION PACKAGE
================================================================================

Supports:
  1. BSR - Building Schedule of Rates (Provincial Councils)
  2. HSR - Highway Schedule of Rates (Road Development Authority - RDA)
  3. Water Supply Rates (National Water Supply & Drainage Board - NWSDB)
  4. Sewerage & Storm Water Drainage Works (Municipal Councils / SLLRDC)

================================================================================
  HOW TO INSTALL ON THIS PC / LAPTOP IN 3 EASY STEPS
================================================================================

STEP 1: PREREQUISITE (DOCKER DESKTOP)
-------------------------------------
Make sure Docker Desktop is installed and running on your Windows PC or laptop.
  - If you already have Docker Desktop: Just ensure it is started (green icon).
  - If you DO NOT have Docker Desktop:
    Download it free from: https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/
    (Or our installer will offer to download it for you automatically).


STEP 2: RUN THE INSTALLER
-------------------------
Choose ONE of the two options below:

  OPTION A: FAST OFFLINE INSTALL (Zero Internet Required - Takes 15 Seconds)
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Double-click:
     install_offline.bat

  This imports the included pre-packaged Docker engines (offline_bundle\docker_images.tar),
  restores all 3,754 rate items, creates your Windows Desktop shortcut,
  and opens the web application immediately!

  OR

  OPTION B: ONLINE WIZARD INSTALLER
  ---------------------------------
  Double-click:
     INSTALL_ON_NEW_PC.bat

  This wizard checks your system, starts Docker, deploys the engines,
  creates the desktop shortcut, configures firewall, and opens the app.


STEP 3: START USING THE SYSTEM!
-------------------------------
Once installed:
  - Local PC Access:  http://localhost:8080
  - Daily Launch:     Double-click the "BSR Rate Hub" icon on your Windows Desktop!
  - Stop the System:  Double-click "stop_windows.bat"


================================================================================
  HOW TO SHARE WITH OTHER PCS / LAPTOPS IN YOUR OFFICE (OFFICE LAN)
================================================================================
You DO NOT need to install anything on your colleagues' laptops!
One PC acts as the server, and everyone in the office can connect via Wi-Fi:

1. On the host PC (where BSR Rate Hub is installed), double-click:
      allow_firewall_lan.bat

2. It will display your PC's network address, for example:
      http://192.168.1.15:8080

3. Any coworker on the same office Wi-Fi can open Chrome or Edge and type
   that address to search rates, compare provincial costs, and create BOQs!
